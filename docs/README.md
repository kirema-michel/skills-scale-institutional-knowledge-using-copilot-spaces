# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory documents how OctoAcme manages cross-functional projects, from validating an idea through delivery and continuous improvement. The approach prioritizes customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, evidence-informed decisions, and psychologically safe feedback.

## Process lifecycle

1. **Initiation:** Confirm the problem, desired outcomes and success measures; align stakeholders; and decide whether the work is ready to plan.
2. **Planning:** Turn approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, milestones, owners, dependencies, and a Definition of Done.
3. **Execution and tracking:** Deliver and test in small increments using the project board, regular team check-ins, reviews, and progress metrics. Keep risks current and escalate blockers through the PM and product lead.
4. **Risk and communication:** Maintain an owned risk register, review it regularly, and share status and decisions with stakeholders on a weekly or milestone-based cadence.
5. **Release and deployment:** Verify acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, prepare release notes and rollback plans, deploy, run post-deployment checks, and communicate the release.
6. **Retrospective and improvement:** After a sprint, release, milestone, or incident, capture learnings and track a small set of owned improvement actions.

Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and communication; Product Managers define outcomes and prioritize work; developers build and test; QA validates quality; and stakeholders provide input and approvals.
